**Document Date:** July 29, 2026

# 1. Introduction

## 1.1 Purpose

The purpose of this Software Requirements Specification is to define the functional and non-functional requirements for a centralized Human Resource Management System.

The system will manage:

* Multiple companies
* Company-wise departments and organizational structures
* Employee records
* Reporting-manager hierarchy
* Shift policies
* Attendance
* Work-hour calculations
* Leave applications and approvals
* Overtime
* Attendance-related deductions
* Work-hour-related deductions
* Payroll generation
* Different payment policies for different employee categories
* Employee organograms
* Payslips and payroll reports

This document will serve as the primary reference for business stakeholders, project managers, UI/UX designers, software developers, database developers, quality assurance engineers, and system administrators.

---

# 2. Project Overview

## 2.1 Product Vision

The proposed HRMS will provide a single platform through which an organization can manage employees from multiple companies while maintaining independent company policies, departments, payroll rules, attendance rules, leave policies, reporting structures, and authorization levels.

The system will automatically calculate employee payroll based on:

* Approved attendance records
* Assigned shift policies
* Required working hours
* Actual working hours
* Late attendance
* Early departure
* Absence
* Approved leave
* Unpaid leave
* Overtime
* Employee payment policy
* Employment type
* Department policy
* Individual employee adjustments

## 2.2 Business Objectives

The system should:

1. Reduce manual HR and payroll processing.
2. Calculate payroll accurately from attendance data.
3. Support multiple companies under one software installation.
4. Support different policies for different employee groups.
5. Automate leave applications and approval routing.
6. Generate employee reporting hierarchies and organograms.
7. Provide complete audit trails for payroll and HR activities.
8. Improve transparency through employee self-service.
9. Prevent unauthorized access to employee and payroll information.
10. Provide management reports for HR, attendance, leave, overtime, and payroll.

# 12. Attendance-Based Deduction

## 12.1 Deduction Conditions

The system shall support salary deductions for:

* Full-day absence
* Half-day absence
* Unpaid leave
* Late arrival
* Early departure
* Insufficient working hours
* Missing attendance
* Unauthorized absence
* Excessive attendance violations

## 12.2 Deduction Methods

The system shall support configurable deduction methods, including:

* Fixed amount
* Daily salary rate
* Hourly salary rate
* Minute-based rate
* Percentage of basic salary
* Percentage of gross salary
* Number of late occurrences
* Accumulated late duration
* Combined late and early-exit duration

## 12.3 Late Deduction Rules

Examples of configurable late policies include:

* First three late arrivals are ignored.
* Every three late arrivals equal one half-day deduction.
* Late duration beyond the grace period is deducted by minutes.
* A late arrival exceeding a configured limit becomes a half-day.
* A late arrival exceeding another configured limit becomes a full-day absence.

These rules shall be configurable and shall not be hard-coded.

## 12.4 Early Departure Rules

The system shall support:

* Grace period
* Minute-based deduction
* Occurrence-based deduction
* Half-day conversion
* Manager-approved exemption

## 12.5 No Double Deduction

The system shall prevent duplicate deduction for the same attendance shortage unless a company explicitly enables combined deductions.

For example, if an employee is already deducted for a half-day absence, the same missing hours should not automatically be deducted again through the work-hour deduction calculation.

# 13. Work-Hour-Based Deduction

## 13.1 Required Work Hours

Required work hours may be determined from:

* Shift duration
* Employee contract
* Daily-hour policy
* Weekly-hour policy
* Monthly target hours
* Employee category
* Department
* Individual employee agreement

## 13.2 Actual Work Hours

Actual work hours shall be calculated from:

* Approved attendance
* Approved attendance corrections
* Paid breaks
* Unpaid breaks
* Approved leave-hour treatment
* Approved official-duty hours

## 13.3 Work-Hour Shortage

The system shall calculate:

**Work-Hour Shortage = Required Work Hours − Eligible Actual Work Hours**

No shortage shall be generated when eligible actual work hours equal or exceed required work hours.

## 13.4 Work-Hour Deduction Formula

The system shall support formulas such as:

**Hourly Rate = Configured Salary Base ÷ Standard Monthly Hours**

**Work-Hour Deduction = Shortage Hours × Hourly Rate**

The salary base may be configured as:

* Basic salary
* Gross salary
* Basic salary plus selected allowances
* Fixed hourly rate
* Category-specific hourly rate

## 13.5 Work-Hour Rules

The system shall support:

* Daily shortage calculation
* Weekly shortage calculation
* Monthly shortage calculation
* Grace shortage hours
* Rounding to configured intervals
* Maximum monthly deduction
* Manager-approved exemption
* Adjustment in the following payroll period

* Fixed amount
* Percentage
* Formula-based calculation
* Attendance dependency
* Taxability indicator

## 16.5 Payroll Calculation

The system shall calculate employee payroll using the following general structure:

**Gross Earnings = Basic Salary + Allowances + Overtime + Bonuses + Incentives + Arrears + Other Earnings**

**Total Deductions = Attendance Deductions + Work-Hour Deductions + Unpaid Leave + Loan Deductions + Tax or Statutory Deductions + Other Deductions**

**Net Payable = Gross Earnings − Total Deductions**

The exact formula shall be configurable by company and employee policy.

## 16.6 Payroll Proration

The system shall support salary proration for:

* New joiners
* Resigned employees
* Terminated employees
* Employees with unpaid leave
* Employees transferred during the period
* Employees whose salary changed during the period
* Employees with contract start or end dates within the period

## 16.7 Payroll Processing Workflow

The payroll workflow shall include:

1. Create payroll period
2. Select company and payroll group
3. Lock attendance input date
4. Load attendance summary
5. Load approved leave
6. Load approved overtime
7. Load recurring salary components
8. Load one-time adjustments
9. Calculate payroll
10. Identify errors and exceptions
11. Review payroll
12. Approve payroll
13. Finalize payroll
14. Generate payslips
15. Export bank payment data
16. Export accounting entries
17. Lock payroll period

## 16.8 Payroll Exceptions

The system shall identify exceptions such as:

* Missing attendance
* Missing salary structure
* Missing bank account
* Negative net salary
* Unapproved overtime
* Unapproved leave
* Overlapping shift assignments
* Duplicate deductions
* Unusual salary variation
* Employee without reporting manager
* Employee assigned to an inactive department

## 16.9 Payroll Adjustment

Authorized users shall be able to add:

* Additional earnings
* Additional deductions
* Attendance corrections
* Overtime corrections
* Arrears
* Retroactive salary adjustments

Every adjustment shall require:

* Adjustment type
* Amount
* Reason
* Effective payroll period
* User information
* Approval history

## 16.10 Payroll Locking

After payroll is finalized:

* Payroll data shall become read-only.
* Changes shall require authorized reopening.
* Reopening shall generate an audit record.
* Historical payslips shall remain unchanged unless payroll is officially recalculated.

# 31. Key Business Rules

1. Every active employee must belong to a company.
2. Every active employee should belong to a department or organizational unit.
3. Every employee should have an immediate reporting manager unless the employee is at the top of the organizational hierarchy.
4. An employee cannot report to themselves.
5. Circular reporting relationships shall not be permitted.
6. Leave requests shall be routed according to the employee’s active reporting relationship.
7. Payroll shall only include approved attendance corrections, leave, and overtime.
8. Finalized payroll shall not be editable without reopening authorization.
9. Policy changes shall be effective-dated.
10. Historical payroll shall retain its original calculation basis.
11. Attendance and work-hour deductions shall not be duplicated unless explicitly configured.
12. Employees shall not be assigned overlapping active primary shifts unless the policy permits split shifts.
13. Leave balance shall be checked before approval unless negative balance or unpaid conversion is allowed.
14. Inactive employees shall not be included in future payroll unless final settlement or special processing is enabled.
15. Salary changes shall require effective dates and authorization.
16. Sensitive salary information shall only be visible to authorized users.
17. Payroll processing shall display exceptions before finalization.
18. Company data shall be logically isolated.
19. Manual attendance changes shall require a reason.
20. Payroll adjustments shall maintain complete approval and audit history.

# 33. Primary System Workflows

## 33.1 Employee Creation Workflow

1. HR creates an employee record.
2. HR assigns the company.
3. HR assigns the department.
4. HR assigns designation and employee category.
5. HR assigns the reporting manager.
6. HR assigns shift and policies.
7. Payroll assigns salary structure.
8. The system creates the employee user account.
9. The employee receives an activation notification.

## 33.2 Leave Workflow

1. Employee opens the leave application.
2. Employee selects the leave type and dates.
3. The system checks eligibility and balance.
4. Employee submits the request.
5. The system identifies the immediate reporting manager.
6. The manager receives a notification.
7. The manager approves, rejects, or returns the request.
8. Additional approval levels are processed where required.
9. The leave balance is updated after final approval.
10. Attendance and payroll receive the approved leave data.

## 33.3 Attendance-to-Payroll Workflow

1. Employee attendance is collected.
2. The assigned shift is identified.
3. Working hours are calculated.
4. Late arrival and early departure are calculated.
5. Leave and holidays are applied.
6. Attendance exceptions are generated.
7. Corrections are submitted and approved.
8. Attendance is locked for payroll.
9. Attendance deductions are calculated.
10. Work-hour shortages are calculated.
11. Approved overtime is calculated.
12. Payroll is generated.
13. Payroll is reviewed and approved.
14. Payslips are generated.

## 33.4 Organogram Workflow

1. HR assigns a reporting manager to each employee.
2. The system validates the hierarchy.
3. The hierarchy is saved with an effective date.
4. The organogram is generated automatically.
5. Changes to reporting relationships update the future organogram while preserving historical relationships.

# 35. Assumptions

1. Every company will nominate authorized HR and payroll users.
2. Employee reporting-manager information will be maintained accurately.
3. Shift, leave, attendance, overtime, and payroll policies will be supplied by the business before final configuration.
4. Statutory payroll rules will be configured according to the operating jurisdiction.
5. Attendance devices will provide usable employee identifiers and timestamps.
6. Bank and accounting integrations will depend on the format supported by the relevant third parties.
7. The system will use effective dates to preserve historical data.
8. The final technology stack and hosting infrastructure will be selected during technical design.

# 39. Conclusion

The proposed system will provide a centralized and configurable HR platform for managing employees across multiple companies and departments.

Its core strength will be the integration between:

* Employee information
* Reporting hierarchy
* Shift policies
* Attendance
* Leave
* Overtime
* Work-hour requirements
* Salary structures
* Payroll calculations

The system will accommodate permanent employees, contractual employees, workers, hourly employees, and other employee categories with different payment and deduction policies.

Payroll will be generated using approved and traceable HR data, while the reporting-manager hierarchy will control leave approvals and automatically generate the organizational organogram.
